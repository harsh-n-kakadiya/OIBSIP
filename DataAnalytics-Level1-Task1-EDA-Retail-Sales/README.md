# Exploratory Data Analysis on Retail Sales Data

## Internship Details

* **Internship Domain:** Data Analytics
* **Organization:** Oasis Infobyte
* **Level:** Level 1
* **Task:** Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on Retail Sales Data

---

## Objective

The objective of this project is to perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a retail sales dataset to discover meaningful patterns, customer behavior, sales trends, and business insights using data visualization techniques.

---

## Tools and Libraries Used

* Python
* Pandas
* NumPy
* Matplotlib
* Seaborn
* Jupyter Notebook

---

## Dataset Description

The dataset contains retail transaction information including:

* Transaction Date
* Customer Age
* Gender
* Product Category
* Quantity Purchased
* Total Amount

The dataset consists of 2000 records collected between 2022 and 2023.

---

## Steps Performed

1. Data Loading and Inspection
2. Data Cleaning and Preparation
3. Descriptive Statistics
4. Monthly and Quarterly Sales Analysis
5. Customer Demographic Analysis
6. Product Category Analysis
7. Correlation Analysis using Heatmap
8. Additional Data Visualization
9. Business Insights and Recommendations

---

## Key Findings

* Total Revenue Generated: **911,720**
* Highest Revenue Category: **Electronics**
* Best Performing Month: **December 2022**
* Best Performing Quarter: **Q4 2022**
* Largest Customer Age Group: **45–54 Years**
* Female customers contributed more transactions.
* Peak sales occurred during evening hours.

---

## Business Recommendations

1. Increase inventory for Electronics products due to high demand.
2. Focus marketing campaigns during high-sales seasons.
3. Introduce personalized offers for customers aged 45–54.
4. Improve promotional activities during low-performing months.
5. Schedule additional staff during peak sales hours.

---

## Conclusion

The analysis provided valuable insights into customer purchasing behavior, sales performance, and revenue trends. These insights can help businesses make informed decisions regarding inventory management, marketing strategies, and customer engagement.

---
